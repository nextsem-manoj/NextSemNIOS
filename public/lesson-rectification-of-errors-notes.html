1. What is a Trial Balance? (तलपट क्या है?)

A Trial Balance is a statement prepared with the debit and credit balances of all ledger accounts on a specific date to verify the arithmetical accuracy of the books of accounts.

तलपट (Trial Balance) एक निश्चित तिथि पर खाता बही (Ledger) के सभी खातों के नाम (Debit) और जमा (Credit) शेषों को दर्शाने वाला एक विवरण-पत्र है, जिसका मुख्य उद्देश्य खातों की अंकगणितीय शुद्धता (Arithmetical Accuracy) की जाँच करना है।

Key Takeaway / मुख्य तथ्य:

• Trial Balance is a statement, NOT an account. (तलपट एक विवरण-पत्र है, खाता नहीं)।

• It is prepared on a specific date, usually at the end of the accounting period.

• If Total Debit = Total Credit, it proves arithmetical accuracy, but does NOT guarantee zero errors!

2. Types of Accounting Errors (अशुद्धियों के 4 मुख्य प्रकार)

Accountancy errors are classified into 4 major categories based on accounting principles and trial balance agreement:

Type of Error (अशुद्धि का प्रकार) Meaning (अर्थ) Example (उदाहरण) Trial Balance Impact
1. Errors of Omission (लोप की अशुद्धियाँ) A transaction is completely or partially omitted from being recorded.
लेनदेन को दर्ज करना भूल जाना।		 Goods sold for ₹5,000 completely omitted from Sales Book.
₹5,000 की बिक्री दर्ज नहीं हुई।		 • Complete: No impact (Agrees)
• Partial: Disagrees
2. Errors of Commission (लिपिकीय / हिसाब की अशुद्धि) Error in posting, casting, carrying forward, or calculation.
गलत खतौनी, गलत योग या गलत शेष निकालना।		 Purchases book undercast by ₹1,000; or ₹540 posted as ₹450.
क्रय बही का योग ₹1,000 कम लगा।		 Disagrees (One-sided errors affect Trial Balance)
3. Errors of Principle (सैद्धांतिक अशुद्धियाँ) Violation of fundamental accounting principles (Capital vs Revenue).
पूँजीगत व आयगत व्यय में भेद न करना।		 Wages paid for installation of Machinery debited to Wages A/c instead of Machinery A/c.
मशीन स्थापना की मजदूरी को मजदूरी खाते में लिखा।		 No impact! Trial Balance will still agree. (तलपट मिल जाएगा)
4. Compensating Errors (क्षतिपूरक अशुद्धियाँ) Two or more independent errors cancel the effect of each other.
एक अशुद्धि दूसरी अशुद्धि के प्रभाव को समाप्त कर दे।		 Ram’s A/c debited with ₹50 less, and Shyam’s A/c debited with ₹50 more.
राम का खाता ₹50 कम, श्याम का ₹50 अधिक नाम हुआ।		 No impact! Trial Balance will still agree.

3. The 3-Step Golden Rule of Rectification (शोधन का 3-चरणीय नियम)

Whenever solving two-sided rectification problems in exams, ALWAYS follow this 3-step magic formula:

Step 1
Correct Entry (सही क्या था?)
Write down the entry that SHOULD HAVE BEEN passed originally according to rules.
Step 2
Wrong Entry (गलत क्या हुआ?)
Write down the incorrect entry that was ACTUALLY recorded in the books.
Step 3
Rectifying Entry (सुधार कैसे करें?)
Reverse the wrong part and apply the correct part to balance the ledger!

⚡ Master Memory Rule for Overcast & Undercast:

• Under-cast (योग कम लगा): Do the SAME (अगर Debit कम है तो Debit करो, Credit कम है तो Credit करो)।

• Over-cast (योग अधिक लगा): REVERSE IT (अगर Debit अधिक है तो Credit करो, Credit अधिक है तो Debit करो)।

4. Suspense Account (उचंत / संशय खाता)

When a Trial Balance does not agree and the accountant cannot locate the errors immediately before final accounts preparation, the difference is temporarily placed into a newly opened dummy account called the Suspense Account.

जब तलपट के दोनों पक्षों का योग बराबर नहीं आता और अंतिम खाते बनाने का समय आ जाता है, तो अंतर की राशि को अस्थायी रूप से एक काल्पनिक खाते में डाल दिया जाता है, जिसे उचंत खाता (Suspense Account) कहते हैं।

Debit Difference in Trial Balance
If Total Debit is LESS than Total Credit, Suspense Account is Debited. It appears on the Assets side of Balance Sheet.
Credit Difference in Trial Balance
If Total Credit is LESS than Total Debit, Suspense Account is Credited. It appears on the Liabilities side of Balance Sheet.

5. High-Yield Solved Cases (CBSE Exam Favorites)

Case 1: Purchases Book Overcast by ₹10,000 (T.S. Grewal Q1)

Reasoning: Purchases Account has a natural Debit balance. Since it is overcast (too much debit by ₹10,000), we must Credit Purchases Account with ₹10,000.

Suspense A/c .................................... Dr. ₹10,000
    To Purchases A/c .................................... ₹10,000
(Being overcasting of purchases book rectified)

Case 2: Installation Wages for Machine ₹2,000 debited to Wages A/c (Error of Principle)

Reasoning: Wages on installation of a fixed asset is a Capital expenditure and should be debited to Machinery A/c, not Wages A/c.

Machinery A/c .................................... Dr. ₹2,000
    To Wages A/c .................................... ₹2,000
(Being installation wages wrongly debited to wages account now rectified)

Case 3: Goods sold to Mohan ₹5,000 recorded in Purchases Book

Reasoning: Mohan should be debited (₹5,000), Sales should be credited (₹5,000), and wrong Purchases debit must be removed by crediting Purchases (₹5,000).

Mohan's A/c .................................... Dr. ₹10,000
    To Sales A/c .................................... ₹5,000
    To Purchases A/c .................................... ₹5,000
(Being credit sales wrongly recorded in purchases book now rectified)
