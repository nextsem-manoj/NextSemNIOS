### 📌 Quick Concept Summary (For Reference)
> **Law of Constant Proportions (Joseph Proust):**  
> *"In a chemical substance (compound), the elements are always present in definite proportions by mass, irrespective of the source or method of preparation."*
> * **Water ($H_2O$):** Mass ratio of $H : O = 1 : 8$  
> * **Ammonia ($NH_3$):** Mass ratio of $N : H = 14 : 3$  
> * **Carbon Monoxide ($CO$):** Mass ratio of $C : O = 3 : 4$  
> * **Sodium Chloride ($NaCl$):** Mass ratio of $Na : Cl = 23 : 35.5$

| **Q1** | Joseph Proust (1799) | In a chemical compound, elements combine in a fixed ratio by mass. |

| **Q3** | $1\text{ g}$ of H, $8\text{ g}$ of O | Total $9\text{ g} = 1\text{ g } H + 8\text{ g } O$. |
| **Q4** | Compound | Definite mass percentage ($86.22\%$ Hg, $13.78\%$ S). |
| **Q5** | **$71\text{ g}$ of Chlorine** | $\text{Mass of } Cl = \frac{35.5}{23} \times 46 = 71\text{ g}$. |
| **Q6** | **$12\text{ g}$ of Oxygen** | $\text{Mass of } O = \frac{4}{3} \times 9 = 12\text{ g}$. |
| **Q7** | **$30\text{ g}$ of Oxygen** | Ratio $S : O = 40 : 60 = 2 : 3$. For $20\text{ g } S \implies \frac{3}{2} \times 20 = 30\text{ g } O$. |
| **Q8** | **$11\text{ g}$ of $CO_2$** | Law of Constant Proportions. Oxygen is in excess ($42\text{ g}$ unreacted). |
| **Q9** | **(a) $4\text{ g } H_2$, (b) $32\text{ g } O_2$** | Ratio $1:8$ (total $9$ parts). $H = \frac{1}{9}\times 36 = 4\text{ g}$, $O = \frac{8}{9}\times 36 = 32\text{ g}$. |
