• KEY ACCOUNTING HINTS & RULES OF DEBIT AND CREDIT •
1. Modern Rules (Dr./Cr.): Assets & Expenses (Increase → Dr., Decrease → Cr.)
2. Modern Rules (Dr./Cr.): Liabilities, Capital & Incomes (Increase → Cr., Decrease → Dr.)
3. Compound Entries: Record multiple accounts affected on the same date into a unified entry.
4. Cash Discounts: Discount Allowed is an expense (Dr.); Discount Received is a revenue/gain (Cr.).

• Examiner Diagnostic Notes & Common Student Pitfalls Marking Scheme: Strict Step-Wise
1. Personal Accounts Settled in Full: Sharma Bros. (₹35,000 settled for ₹34,000) and Gupta Traders (₹20,000 settled for ₹19,500) have ZERO closing balances. Do NOT list them in the Trial Balance!
2. Discount Treatment: Discount Received (₹1,000) is Income → Credit side of TB. Discount Allowed (₹500) is Expense → Debit side of TB. Confusing these two distorts the trial balance by ₹1,000.
3. Drawings vs Capital: Drawings (₹6,000) must appear in the Debit column as a deduction from owner's equity. Capital (₹1,20,000) must appear in the Credit column.
4. Mathematical Verification: Trial Balance agreement: Debit Total = ₹60,000 + ₹20,500 + ₹6,000 + ₹15,000 + ₹60,000 + ₹500 + ₹5,000 + ₹4,000 = ₹2,06,000. Credit Total = ₹1,20,000 + ₹50,000 + ₹1,000 = ₹2,06,000.
