• KEY ACCOUNTING HINTS & RULES OF DEBIT AND CREDIT •
1. Modern Rules (Dr./Cr.): Assets & Expenses (Increase → Dr., Decrease → Cr.)
2. Modern Rules (Dr./Cr.): Liabilities, Capital & Incomes (Increase → Cr., Decrease → Dr.)
3. Compound Entries: Record multiple accounts affected on the same date into a unified entry.
4. Cash Discounts: Discount Allowed is an expense (Dr.); Discount Received is a revenue/gain (Cr.).
