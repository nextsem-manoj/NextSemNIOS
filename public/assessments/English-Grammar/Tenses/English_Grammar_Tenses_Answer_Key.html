Section A Tense Identification & Classification Solutions
10 Marks [10 × 1]
Award 1 full mark per correct tense name. Do not penalize if the word "Tense" is omitted.
1. The children are playing hide-and-seek in the orchard.
Correct Answer: Present Continuous Tense
2. Pure water freezes at zero degrees Celsius.
Correct Answer: Simple Present Tense
3. By sunset, the mountaineers had reached base camp.
Correct Answer: Past Perfect Tense
4. We will be flying over the Alps at noon tomorrow.
Correct Answer: Future Continuous Tense
5. The author penned an award-winning historical novel.
Correct Answer: Simple Past Tense
6. Evelyn has been practicing classical violin since 2021.
Correct Answer: Present Perfect Continuous
7. NASA will launch the deep-space observatory next year.
Correct Answer: Simple Future Tense
8. While father was cooking dinner, the telephone rang.
Correct Answer: Past Continuous Tense
9. The robotics team has designed a self-balancing rover.
Correct Answer: Present Perfect Tense
10. Before the bell, they had been writing for two hours.
Correct Answer: Past Perfect Continuous
Section B Conjugation & Fill in the Blanks Solutions
10 Marks [10 × 1]
Award 1 mark each. Deduct 0.5 mark for spelling errors if grammatical form is intact.
1. The Earth revolves around the Sun continuously. (Universal truth / habit)
2. Look at the dark sky! It is going to pour / will pour rain any minute now.
3. Yesterday at 5 PM, Liam was painting the wooden fence. (Action in progress)
4. Alexander Fleming discovered penicillin in 1928. (Definite past year)
5. I cannot find my glasses; I think I have misplaced them. (Present result)
6. By next December, they will have completed the highway. (Future Perfect)
7. We had waited / had been waiting forty minutes before train arrived.
8. If you study regularly, you will achieve top marks. (1st conditional)
9. The morning express train rarely arrives behind schedule. (Timetable)
10. She was exhausted because she had been working since 6 AM.
Section C Error Spotting & Correction Solutions
5 Marks [5 × 1]
Award 0.5 mark for identifying incorrect form + 0.5 mark for providing the correct form.
Given Sentence (with grammatical error) Identified Error Corrected Verb Form & Rule Rationale
1. Rohan is knowing the answer to every tricky science riddle. is knowing knows (Stative verb: no continuous aspect)
2. Last Sunday afternoon, we have visited the observatory. have visited visited (Specific past time marker 'Last Sunday')
3. The school bell rang after all students left the campus. left had left (Earlier of two past events ➔ Past Perfect)
4. Mr. Harrison lives in Edinburgh since November 2015. lives has lived / has been living ('Since' duration)
5. They will arriving at Tokyo Airport by 8:00 tonight. will arriving will arrive / will be arriving (Modal + bare base)
Teacher Rubric Pedagogical Note: Ensure students understand the fundamental distinction between completed past events versus ongoing states connected to the present.

Section D Sentence Transformation Model Answers
10 Marks [5 × 2]
Award 2 marks for complete, grammatically sound sentence with correct auxiliary and participle. Deduct 1 mark if minor punctuation error.
1. The master chef prepares authentic Italian pasta for the foreign guests. Present Perfect Tense
Model Solution: The master chef has prepared authentic Italian pasta for the foreign guests. (has + prepared)
2. The construction workers repaired the highway bridge during heavy traffic. Future Continuous Tense
Model Solution: The construction workers will be repairing the highway bridge during heavy traffic. (will be + repairing)
3. The curious tourists bought handwoven rugs before taking the evening train. Past Perfect Tense
Model Solution: The curious tourists had bought handwoven rugs before taking the evening train. (had + bought)
4. Lucas conducts marine research on coral reefs along the barrier coastline. Present Perfect Continuous Tense
Model Solution: Lucas has been conducting marine research on coral reefs along the barrier coastline. (has been + conducting)
5. The supersonic passenger aircraft landed safely at the international terminal. Simple Future Tense (with 'tomorrow')
Model Solution: The supersonic passenger aircraft will land safely at the international terminal tomorrow. (will land)

The Discovery of the Sandstone Vault:
Last summer, Dr. Evans and his archaeological team (1) traveled to Petra. For decades, historians (2) had believed a royal library lay hidden. While the crew (3) were excavating the eastern trench, a volunteer (4) uncovered a bronze door. Prior to that, no modern human (5) had entered the sanctum. “We (6) have been searching since 2012,” he declared. Presently, the team (7) is analyzing scrolls. By December, curators (8) will have published findings. Tomorrow, new crew (9) will arrive, and everyone hopes this (10) will inspire future explorations.

Section F Creative Composition Grading Rubric & Samples

Exemplar Student Sentence: “The rescue team had been searching the alpine ridge for seven hours before the heavy blizzard forced them to return to base.”
2. Future Perfect Tense: Completed achievement before deadline (will have + past participle with by next...): 2.5 Marks
Exemplar Student Sentence: “By next summer, the environmental institute will have planted fifty thousand native trees across the valley.”
