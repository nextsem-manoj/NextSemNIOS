⚖️ The 3 Golden Rules of Accounting

The timeless British traditional approach based on the nature of accounts.

👤

1. Personal Account

Accounts of persons, firms, companies, creditors, debtors, bank, capital, and drawings.

Debit Rule
Debit the Receiver
Credit Rule
Credit the Giver
  • Ram A/c
  • SBI Bank A/c
  • Capital A/c
  • Drawings A/c
🏢

2. Real Account

Accounts of tangible & intangible properties and assets owned by the enterprise.

Debit Rule
Debit what comes in
Credit Rule
Credit what goes out
  • Cash A/c
  • Machinery A/c
  • Furniture A/c
  • Building A/c
📊

3. Nominal Account

Accounts relating to expenses, losses, revenues, incomes, and business profits.

Debit Rule
Debit all expenses & losses
Credit Rule
Credit all incomes & gains
  • Purchases A/c
  • Sales A/c
  • Rent Paid A/c
  • Discount Recd A/c

⚡ Modern (American) Approach: DEAL & CLEAR

Classify accounts by their balance behavior.

Debit Normal Balance (D.E.A.L.)

These accounts increase on DEBIT and decrease on CREDIT.

Drawings
Owner withdrawals
+ Debit / - Credit
Expenses & Losses
Rent, Purchases, Cartage
+ Debit / - Credit
Assets
Cash, Bank, Debtors, Stock
+ Debit / - Credit

Credit Normal Balance (C.L.E.A.R.)

These accounts increase on CREDIT and decrease on DEBIT.

Capital / Equity
Owner funds invested
+ Credit / - Debit
Liabilities
Creditors, Loans, Overdraft
+ Credit / - Debit
Revenue & Gains
Sales, Interest Earned
+ Credit / - Debit

💡 Special & Exam-Critical Transactions

Concepts that frequently appear in Class 11 and professional accounting exams.

🏷️

Trade Discount vs. Cash Discount

Trade Discount: Allowed on catalog price for bulk volume. Never recorded in journal entries! Directly subtracted from invoice.

Cash Discount: Allowed for prompt/early payment. Always recorded as Discount Allowed (Dr.) or Discount Received (Cr.).

📉

Bad Debts & Bad Debts Recovered

When customer is bankrupt (Irrecoverable):
Bad Debts A/c (Dr.) To Customer's A/c (Cr.)

When bad debt is recovered later:
Cash A/c (Dr.) To Bad Debts Recovered A/c (Cr.) (Do not credit customer!)
