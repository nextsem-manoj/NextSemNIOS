📌 Law of Constant Proportions (Joseph Proust, 1799)

"In a chemical substance (compound), the elements are always present in definite proportions by mass, irrespective of its source or method of preparation."

Water (H₂O) → H : O = 1 : 8
Ammonia (NH₃) → N : H = 14 : 3
Carbon Monoxide (CO) → C : O = 3 : 4
Salt (NaCl) → Na : Cl = 23 : 35.5
