📌 Law of Conservation of Mass (Antoine Lavoisier, 1789)

"Matter can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. In every reaction, the total mass of the reactants equals the total mass of the products."

Formula: Total Mass (Reactants) = Total Mass (Products)
Closed System: Mass Conserved Exactly
Barium Sulfate: White Precipitate (BaSO₄)
Dalton's Postulate: Atoms are Indivisible
