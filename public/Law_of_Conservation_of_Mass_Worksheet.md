### 📌 Quick Concept Summary (For Reference)
> **Law of Conservation of Mass (Antoine Lavoisier, 1789):**  
> *"Matter can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. In every chemical change, the total mass of the reactants is always equal to the total mass of the products."*
> $$\text{Total Mass of Reactants} = \text{Total Mass of Products}$$
> * **Closed System:** In a sealed system (e.g., conical flask sealed with a balloon), all products (including gases like $CO_2$ or $H_2$) are retained, so mass before = mass after.
> * **Open System Apparent Loss:** When reactions produce gases in open beakers (e.g., combustion of ethanol, decomposition of carbonates), gases escape into the air, giving an apparent mass decrease.
> * **Dalton's Atomic Theory Link:** Dalton explained this law by stating: *"Atoms are indivisible particles that cannot be created or destroyed in a chemical reaction."*
