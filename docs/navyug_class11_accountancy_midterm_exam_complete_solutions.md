## 📋 Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (Q1: 20 Marks — 1 Mark Each)

### Q1 (i)
**Question:** Under Cash Basis of Accounting, only cash transactions are recorded. So transactions relating to:
- (A) Cash
- (B) Bank
- (C) Credit
- (D) A and B
- **Correct Answer:** **(D) A and B**
- **Explanation:** Cash basis of accounting strictly records receipts and payments realized in currency cash or processed via bank channels. Credit transactions are recognized only when cash/cheque is actually received or disbursed.

### Q1 (ii)
**Question:** Goods given as free sample is credited to:
- (A) Advertisement A/c
- (B) Purchases A/c
- (C) Sales A/c
- (D) None of these
- **Correct Answer:** **(B) Purchases A/c**
- **Journal Entry:** `Advertisement A/c Dr. / To Purchases A/c`
- **Explanation:** Samples are distributed for sales promotion at cost price. As goods are leaving the business without a revenue-generating sale, the net balance of Purchases is reduced.

### Q1 (iii)
**Question:** Goodwill Account is a:
- (A) Personal account
- (B) Real account
- (C) Nominal account
- (D) None of these
- **Correct Answer:** **(B) Real account**
- **Explanation:** Real accounts represent tangible and intangible assets/properties owned by the enterprise. Goodwill is an intangible real asset with definite commercial value.

### Q1 (iv)
**Question:** Drawings of cash is credited to:
- (A) Capital A/c
- (B) Drawings A/c
- (C) Cash A/c
- (D) Purchases A/c
- **Correct Answer:** **(C) Cash A/c**
- **Journal Entry:** `Drawings A/c Dr. / To Cash A/c`
- **Explanation:** Following the rule "Credit what goes out" (or modern rule: Credit decrease in Asset), Cash Account is credited.

### Q1 (v)
**Question:** "Debit the receiver, Credit the giver" rule is applied to:
- (A) Real A/c
- (B) Nominal A/c
- (C) Personal A/c
- (D) None of these
- **Correct Answer:** **(C) Personal A/c**
- **Explanation:** This is the classical golden rule governing Personal Accounts (persons, firms, debtor/creditor entities).

### Q1 (vi)
**Question:** The primary qualities that make accounting information useful for decision-making are:
- (A) Reliability and Relevance
- (B) Understandability and Comparability
- (C) Both (A) and (B)
- (D) None of these
- **Correct Answer:** **(C) Both (A) and (B)**
- **Explanation:** The four fundamental qualitative pillars under ICAI/IASB frameworks are Reliability, Relevance, Understandability, and Comparability.

### Q1 (vii)
**Question:** When goods are purchased on credit from Mohan, the entry is:
- (A) Purchases A/c Dr. To Mohan
- (B) Mohan Dr. To Purchases A/c
- (C) Purchases A/c Dr. To Cash A/c
- (D) Cash A/c Dr. To Mohan
- **Correct Answer:** **(A) Purchases A/c Dr. To Mohan**
- **Explanation:** Purchases represents an increase in expense (Dr.), and Mohan becomes a creditor, representing an increase in liability (Cr.).

### Q1 (viii)
**Question:** Payment made to a creditor causes:
- (A) Increase in Assets and decrease in Liabilities
- (B) Decrease in Assets and decrease in Liabilities
- (C) Decrease in Assets and increase in Capital
- (D) No change in Assets or Liabilities
- **Correct Answer:** **(B) Decrease in Assets and decrease in Liabilities**
- **Explanation:** Cash is reduced (Asset $\downarrow$) and the amount owed to creditors is cleared (Liability $\downarrow$).

### Q1 (ix)
**Question:** If Opening Capital is ₹ 60,000, Additional Capital is ₹ 10,000, Profit during the year is ₹ 20,000 and Drawings are ₹ 5,000, what is the Closing Capital?
- (A) ₹ 85,000
- (B) ₹ 75,000
- (C) ₹ 95,000
- (D) ₹ 80,000
- **Correct Answer:** **(A) ₹ 85,000**
- **Calculation:** $\text{Closing Capital} = 60,000 + 10,000 + 20,000 - 5,000 = \text{₹ } 85,000$.

### Q1 (x)
**Question:** Which basis of accounting is recognized under the Companies Act, 2013?
- (A) Cash Basis
- (B) Accrual Basis of Accounting
- (C) Both (A) and (B)
- (D) Hybrid Basis
- **Correct Answer:** **(B) Accrual Basis of Accounting**
- **Explanation:** Section 128(1) of the Companies Act, 2013 legally mandates that all companies maintain their books on Accrual Basis using the double-entry system.

### Q1 (xi)

## 📑 Section C: Conceptual Theory & Debit Vouchers (Q5 - Q7)

### Q5. Explain Any Two Accounting Concepts (4 Marks)
1. **Money Measurement Concept:** Only transactions capable of being quantified in monetary terms are recorded. Qualitative organizational strengths are excluded.
2. **Dual Aspect Concept:** Every transaction has a twofold effect (Debit = Credit). Governed by $\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Capital}$.
3. **Business Entity Concept:** The business enterprise is a distinct legal and economic unit separate from the proprietor. Capital is viewed as an internal liability.

---

### Q6. Meaning & Limitations of Accounting (4 Marks)
- **Meaning:** Accounting is the systematic art of identifying, recording, classifying, summarizing, and reporting financial data in monetary terms for economic decision-making.
- **Three Limitations:**
  1. *Omission of qualitative information* (e.g. employee skills, goodwill morale).
  2. *Historical cost valuation* ignores inflation and purchasing power adjustments.
  3. *Subject to bias & window dressing* in depreciation lifespans and bad debt estimates.
