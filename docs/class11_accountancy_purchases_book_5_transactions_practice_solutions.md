## 📌 Fundamental Golden Rules of the Purchases Book

> ### ⚠️ The 2 Non-Negotiable Filter Rules:
> 1. **ONLY Credit Purchases of GOODS are recorded.** (Goods = items bought for the purpose of resale or core manufacturing).
> 2. **CASH purchases are STRICTLY EXCLUDED** $\rightarrow$ Recorded in the **Cash Book**.
> 3. **Credit purchases of ASSETS/EQUIPMENT are STRICTLY EXCLUDED** $\rightarrow$ Recorded in the **Journal Proper**.
> 4. **Trade Discount** is deducted immediately from the gross amount before calculating GST. (Trade discount is *never* recorded in ledger accounts).

#### **Transaction 1 (April 04, 2026):**

## 🧮 Step-by-Step Working Notes & Tax Calculations

### 1. Working Note for April 04 (M/s Modern Electronics):
- 20 Laptops @ ₹30,000 = ₹6,00,000
- 15 Desktop Monitors @ ₹8,000 = ₹1,20,000
- **Gross Invoice Value** = ₹7,20,000
- *Less:* Trade Discount (10% on ₹7,20,000) = **(-) ₹72,000**
- **Net Cost / Taxable Value** = **₹6,48,000**
- *Add:* Input CGST (9% of ₹6,48,000) = **₹58,320**
- *Add:* Input SGST (9% of ₹6,48,000) = **₹58,320**
- **Total Amount Payable** = ₹6,48,000 + ₹58,320 + ₹58,320 = **₹7,64,640**

---

### 2. Working Note for April 21 (M/s Galaxy Silicon Tech — Inter-State):
- 40 External SSDs @ ₹4,000 = ₹1,60,000
- 50 Wireless Keyboards @ ₹2,000 = ₹1,00,000
- **Gross Invoice Value** = ₹2,60,000
- *Less:* Trade Discount (15% on ₹2,60,000) = **(-) ₹39,000**
- **Net Cost / Taxable Value** = **₹2,21,000**
- *Add:* Input IGST (18% of ₹2,21,000) = **₹39,780**
- *Add:* Freight / Delivery Charges = **₹2,000**
- **Total Amount Payable** = ₹2,21,000 + ₹39,780 + ₹2,000 = **₹2,62,780**

---

### 3. Working Note for April 28 (M/s Delhi Digital Store):
- 30 Webcams @ ₹3,000 = ₹90,000
- 25 Gaming Headsets @ ₹2,000 = ₹50,000
- **Gross Invoice Value** = ₹1,40,000
- *Less:* Trade Discount (20% on ₹1,40,000) = **(-) ₹28,000**
- **Net Cost / Taxable Value** = **₹1,12,000**
- *Add:* Input CGST (9% of ₹1,12,000) = **₹10,080**
- *Add:* Input SGST (9% of ₹1,12,000) = **₹10,080**
- **Total Amount Payable** = ₹1,12,000 + ₹10,080 + ₹10,080 = **₹1,32,160**

## 🎯 Key Takeaways for Examinations & Real-Life Bookkeeping
1. **Double Entry Equilibrium Check:**
   $$\text{Total Debits} = ₹9,81,000 (\text{Purchases}) + ₹68,400 (\text{CGST}) + ₹68,400 (\text{SGST}) + ₹39,780 (\text{IGST}) + ₹2,000 (\text{Freight}) = \mathbf{₹11,59,580}$$
   $$\text{Total Credits} = ₹7,64,640 + ₹2,62,780 + ₹1,32,160 = \mathbf{₹11,59,580}$$
2. **Timing of Postings:**
   - **Individual Creditors' Accounts:** Posted on a **daily / transaction basis** (Credit side).
   - **Purchases & Tax Accounts:** Posted as a **monthly periodic total** on the last day of the month (Debit side).
