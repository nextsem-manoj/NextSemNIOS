### **Question 8** [2 Marks]
**Calculate the enthalpy change for the following reaction:**
$$2\text{H}_2(\text{g}) + \text{O}_2(\text{g}) \to 2\text{H}_2\text{O}(\text{g})$$
**Given bond energies:**
- $\text{Bond energy of H}-\text{H bond} = 436\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$
- $\text{Bond energy of O}-\text{H bond} = 423\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$
- $\text{Bond energy of O}=\text{O bond} = 496.4\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$

> 💡 **Hint:** Enthalpy of reaction using bond energies:
> $$\Delta H_{\text{rxn}} = \sum (\text{Bond energies of reactants broken}) - \sum (\text{Bond energies of products formed})$$

* **Teacher's Foundation (Part Answer):**
  - **Step 1: Energy required to break bonds in reactants:**
    - Breaking 2 moles of $\text{H}-\text{H}$ bonds $= 2 \times 436 = 872\text{ kJ}$
    - Breaking 1 mole of $\text{O}=\text{O}$ bond $= 1 \times 496.4 = 496.4\text{ kJ}$
    - Total energy absorbed $= 872 + 496.4 = 1368.4\text{ kJ}$
  - **Step 2: Energy released upon forming bonds in products:**
    - Each $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ has $2 \times (\text{O}-\text{H})$ bonds.
    - $2\text{ moles of H}_2\text{O}$ contain $2 \times 2 = 4\text{ moles of O}-\text{H}$ bonds.
    - Total energy released $= 4 \times 423\text{ kJ mol}^{-1} = 1692\text{ kJ}$

* **Your Turn (Complete the Answer):**
  - Calculate $\Delta H_{\text{rxn}}$:
    $$\Delta H_{\text{rxn}} = \text{Total energy absorbed} - \text{Total energy released}$$
    $$\Delta H_{\text{rxn}} = 1368.4\text{ kJ} - \underline{\hspace{2.5cm}}\text{ kJ} = \underline{\hspace{3cm}}\text{ kJ}$$
  - Since $\Delta H$ has a negative sign, the reaction is $\underline{\hspace{3.5cm}}$ (exothermic / endothermic).
