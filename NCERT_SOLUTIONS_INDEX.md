## 🏫 Senior Secondary Stage (Classes 11 & 12)

### 📈 Economics & Statistics (Class 11 & 12)
- **Class 11 Statistics for Economics (`kest1dd`)**:
  - Ch 1: Introduction (Statistics definition, scope & functions)
  - Ch 2: Collection of Data (Primary vs secondary, census vs sampling)
  - Ch 3: Organisation of Data (Classification, frequency distribution)
  - Ch 4: Presentation of Data (Tabular, bar diagram, pie chart, histogram)
  - *Embedded Test Suite:* **Statistics Chapters 1–4 Mega Test (30 MCQs with Hinglish explanations)**
- **Class 11 Introductory Microeconomics (`keec1dd`)**:
  - Consumer Equilibrium, Demand, Production Function, Cost, Revenue, Supply, Market Equilibrium.

### 🏛️ History (Class 11 & 12)
- **Class 11 World History (`khhs1dd`)**:
  - Ch 2: Writing and City Life (Mesopotamia)
  - Ch 3: An Empire Across Three Continents (Roman Empire)
  - Ch 5: Nomadic Empires (`khhs105` - Genghis Khan, Yasa, Yam system)
  - Ch 6: The Three Orders (`khhs104` - Feudalism, Clergy, Nobility, Peasantry)
  - Ch 7: Changing Cultural Traditions
  - Ch 8: Displacing Indigenous Peoples
  - Ch 9: Paths to Modernisation
- **Class 12 Themes in Indian History (`lhhs1/2/3`)**:
  - Part I: Bricks, Beads and Bones; Kings, Farmers and Towns; Kinship, Caste and Class; Thinkers, Beliefs and Buildings.
  - Part II: Through the Eyes of Travellers (`lhhs201`); Bhakti–Sufi Traditions (`lhhs202` - Alvars, Nayanars, Kabir, Mirabai, Guru Nanak); An Imperial Capital Vijayanagara; Peasants, Zamindars and the State.
  - Part III: Colonialism and the Countryside; Rebels and the Raj; Mahatma Gandhi and Nationalist Movement; Framing the Constitution.
  - *Embedded Test Suite:* **History Bhakti–Sufi & Mongols Competency Test (Dynamic Shuffling)**

### ⚛️ Physics (Class 11 & 12)
- **Class 11 (`keph1/2`)**: Units & Measurements, Motion in a Straight Line, Motion in a Plane, Laws of Motion, Work Energy & Power, System of Particles, Gravitation, Mechanical Properties, Thermodynamics, Waves.
- **Class 12 (`leph1/2`)**:
  - Electric Charges & Fields, Electrostatic Potential, Current Electricity, Moving Charges, Magnetism.
  - Ch 1: Electric Charges & Fields (`leph101` - *All 14 Derivations Master Studio with Vector Diagrams & CBSE Rubrics*)
  - Ch 2: Electrostatic Potential & Capacitance (`leph102` - *All 18 Derivations Master Studio with Diagrams & CBSE Rubrics*)
  - Ch 6: Electromagnetic Induction (`leph106` - EMI Formula Vault & Numericals)
  - Ch 7: Alternating Current (`leph107` - 6 Difficulty Tiers & Practice)
  - Ray Optics, Wave Optics, Dual Nature, Atoms, Nuclei, Semiconductor Devices.

### 🧪 Chemistry (Class 11 & 12) & NIOS Chemistry (313)
