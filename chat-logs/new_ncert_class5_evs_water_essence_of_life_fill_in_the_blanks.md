## 🎯 Key Content & Structure Implemented
The module covers the entire textbook chapter line-by-line across 10 engaging story sections with **55 interactive blanks**, each featuring an expandable `💡 Hint` and `👁️ Ans` pill:

1. **Section 1: Afreen & Jyoti’s Kitchen Observation & The Three States of Water** (Blanks 1–6)
   - Ice melts to water, steam rises upon boiling, solid/liquid/gas states, condensation experiment with cold metal lid.
2. **Section 2: The Magnificent Journey of Water — The Water Cycle** (Blanks 7–12)
   - Sun warms water bodies, evaporation into water vapour, cooling and condensation into droplets, cloud formation, precipitation (rain & snow), replenishment.
3. **Section 3: India's Diverse Freshwater Bodies & Famous Lakes** (Blanks 13–18)
   - Rivers, ponds, lakes, natural springs; Dal Lake and Wular Lake in Kashmir; Chilika Lake in Odisha; saline seawater vs freshwater necessity.
4. **Section 4: The Mystery of Groundwater, Wells & Aquifers** (Blanks 19–24)
   - Rainwater seeps through soil into aquifers (permeable layers of gravel, sand, limestone); dug wells, hand pumps, tube wells, water table fluctuations.
5. **Section 5: Traditional Water Conservation & Stepwells (Baolis)** (Blanks 25–29)
   - Ancient rainwater harvesting; stepwells/baolis across Rajasthan and Gujarat; communal water heritage and cool community meeting points.
6. **Section 6: The Mustard Seed & Water Slope Experiment** (Blanks 30–35)
   - Tray experiment demonstrating river velocity, steeper slopes carry water faster, sediment and gravel transport, formation of bends/meanders.
7. **Section 7: Amazing Aquatic Life & Plant Adaptations** (Blanks 36–41)
   - Fish breathing dissolved oxygen via gills; streamlined bodies; water lilies/lotus with waxy leaves and flexible air-filled stems; floating duckweed.
8. **Section 8: Keibul Lamjao National Park & The Floating Phumdis of Loktak Lake** (Blanks 42–46)
   - Manipur's Loktak lake with floating biomass islands called *phumdis*; home of the endangered Sangai (brow-antlered dancing deer).
9. **Section 9: Community Water Regeneration — The Inspiring Hiware Bazar Story** (Blanks 47–51)
   - Drought-prone village in Maharashtra transforming into water-abundant oasis through watershed management, bunds, percolation tanks, and ban on water-intensive crops.
10. **Section 10: Protecting Our Water Heritage & The Jal Shakti Mission** (Blanks 52–55)
    - Preventing industrial discharge and plastic dumping; Jal Jeevan Mission / Jal Shakti Ministry initiatives; individual conservation habits.
