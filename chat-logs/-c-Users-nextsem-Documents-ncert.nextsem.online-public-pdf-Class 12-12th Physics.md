### 📐 Comprehensive List of All 18 Derivations Included

#### **Part A: Electrostatic Potential (NCERT Sections 2.2 – 2.6.1)**
1. **Derivation 1 — Potential due to an Isolated Point Charge:**
   $$V(r) = -\int_\infty^r \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{r} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{q}{r}$$
2. **Derivation 2 — Potential due to an Electric Dipole at Arbitrary Point $(r, \theta)$:**
   $$V(r, \theta) = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{p\cos\theta}{r^2} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{\vec{p}\cdot\hat{r}}{r^2}$$
   - *Case I (Axial):* $V = \pm\frac{p}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}$
   - *Case II (Equatorial):* $V = 0$
3. **Derivation 3 — Potential due to System of $N$ Point Charges & Charged Spherical Shell:**
   $$V(P) = \sum_{i=1}^N \frac{q_i}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r_i} \quad \text{and inside shell } (r \le R): V = \frac{q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 R}$$
4. **Derivation 4 — Relation between Electric Field and Potential Gradient:**
   $$E = -\frac{dV}{dl} \quad \text{and} \quad \vec{E} = -\vec{\nabla} V$$

#### **Part B: Electrostatic Potential Energy (NCERT Sections 2.7 – 2.8.3)**
5. **Derivation 5 — Potential Energy of Two Point Charges (No External Field):**
   $$U = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{q_1 q_2}{r_{12}}$$
6. **Derivation 6 — Potential Energy of Three Point Charges:**
   $$U = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \left[ \frac{q_1 q_2}{r_{12}} + \frac{q_2 q_3}{r_{23}} + \frac{q_1 q_3}{r_{13}} \right]$$
7. **Derivation 7 — Potential Energy of Two Charges in External Electric Field:**
   $$U = q_1 V(\vec{r}_1) + q_2 V(\vec{r}_2) + \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{q_1 q_2}{r_{12}}$$
8. **Derivation 8 — Potential Energy of an Electric Dipole in Uniform External Field:**
   $$U(\theta) = -\vec{p}\cdot\vec{E} = -p E \cos\theta$$
   - *Stable Equilibrium ($\theta = 0^\circ$):* $U = -pE$
   - *Unstable Equilibrium ($\theta = 180^\circ$):* $U = +pE$

#### **Part C: Conductors & Dielectrics (NCERT Sections 2.9 – 2.10)**
9. **Derivation 9 — Electric Field at Surface of Charged Conductor via Gaussian Pillbox:**
   $$\vec{E} = \frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}\hat{n}$$
10. **Derivation 10 — Capacitance of an Isolated Spherical Conductor:**
    $$C = 4\pi\varepsilon_0 R \quad (C = 4\pi K\varepsilon_0 R \text{ in dielectric medium})$$

#### **Part D: Capacitors & Combinations (NCERT Sections 2.12 – 2.14.2)**
11. **Derivation 11 — Parallel Plate Capacitor with Air/Vacuum Dielectric:**
    $$C_0 = \frac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d}$$
12. **Derivation 12 — Parallel Plate Capacitor Completely Filled with Dielectric:**
    $$C = \frac{K \varepsilon_0 A}{d} = K C_0$$
13. **Derivation 13 — Parallel Plate Capacitor with Partially Filled Dielectric Slab ($t < d$):**
    $$C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d - t\left(1 - \frac{1}{K}\right)} \quad \left(\text{For conducting slab: } C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d - t}\right)$$
14. **Derivation 14 — Equivalent Capacitance of Capacitors in Series:**
    $$\frac{1}{C_{\text{eq}}} = \frac{1}{C_1} + \frac{1}{C_2} + \dots + \frac{1}{C_n}$$
15. **Derivation 15 — Equivalent Capacitance of Capacitors in Parallel:**
    $$C_{\text{eq}} = C_1 + C_2 + \dots + C_n$$

#### **Part E: Energy Stored & Sharing of Charges (NCERT Section 2.15)**
16. **Derivation 16 — Electrostatic Potential Energy Stored in Charged Capacitor:**
    $$U = \int_0^Q \frac{q'}{C} dq' = \frac{Q^2}{2C} = \frac{1}{2} C V^2 = \frac{1}{2} Q V$$
17. **Derivation 17 — Electrostatic Energy Density of an Electric Field:**
    $$u_E = \frac{U}{\text{Volume}} = \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0 E^2 \quad \left(u_E = \frac{1}{2}K\varepsilon_0 E^2 \text{ in dielectric}\right)$$
18. **Derivation 18 — Common Potential & Energy Loss on Sharing Charges Between Capacitors:**
    $$V_c = \frac{C_1 V_1 + C_2 V_2}{C_1 + C_2}, \quad \Delta U = \frac{1}{2}\frac{C_1 C_2}{C_1 + C_2}(V_1 - V_2)^2 \ge 0$$
