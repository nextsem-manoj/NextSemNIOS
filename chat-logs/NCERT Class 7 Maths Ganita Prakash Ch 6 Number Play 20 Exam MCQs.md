## 📑 Core Chapter Concepts Covered (from `gecu106.pdf`)

1. **Adolescence & Puberty (किशोरवय एवं यौवनारंभ):**
   - Growth period connecting childhood to adulthood (**10 to 19 years of age**).
   - Puberty onset: Growth spurt, physical changes, and maturation of reproductive organs.
2. **Physical & Physiological Changes:**
   - **Adam’s Apple (कंठमणि / लैरिंक्स):** Voice box protrusion in boys leading to deepening/cracking of voice.
   - **Sweat & Sebaceous Glands:** Hyperactivity in skin oil glands causing teenage acne/pimples (Ramesh's case).
   - **Secondary Sexual Characteristics:** Facial hair, chest broadening in boys; breast development, pelvic broadening in girls.
3. **Endocrine Hormones & Chemical Messengers:**
   - Pituitary gland (Master gland in the brain) releasing stimulating hormones to trigger sex hormones (Testosterone & Estrogen).
4. **Reproductive Health & Menstrual Cycle:**
   - 28–30 day natural biological cycle (healthy variation: 21–35 days; menopause at 45–55 years).
   - **Menstrual Hygiene Scheme (MHS)** & **Suvidha Eco-friendly Oxo-biodegradable sanitary pads** under PMBJP at ₹1/pad.
5. **Nutritional Requirements & Iron-Rich Diet:**
   - Prevention of **Anaemia (रक्ताल्पता)** with jaggery (गुड़), green leafy vegetables, amla, meat, and iron supplements.
   - Dorothy Crowfoot Hodgkin's landmark discovery: Structure of **Vitamin B12** (1964 Nobel Prize in Chemistry).
6. **Mental Health, Substance Abuse & Social Media Awareness:**
   - Coping with emotional swings and peer pressure.
   - **Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (NMBA)** & National Drug De-addiction Helpline: **14446**.
   - Digital hygiene, cyber safety, screen time management, and privacy protection.
