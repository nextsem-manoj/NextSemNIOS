## ⚗️ All 4 Methods of Preparation of Alkanes Included:

### 1. From Haloalkanes (Alkyl Halides, R-X)
- **(a) Reduction of Haloalkanes**:
  - *(i) Zn + dil. HCl*: $\text{CH}_3\text{Br} + \text{Zn} + 2\text{HCl} \to \text{CH}_4 + \text{ZnCl}_2 + \text{HBr}$
  - *(ii) HI / Red Phosphorus (423 K)*: $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{I} + \text{HI} \xrightarrow{\text{Red P / 423 K}} \text{C}_2\text{H}_6 + \text{I}_2$
  - *(iii) Catalytic Reduction*: $\text{CH}_3\text{Cl} + \text{H}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{Pt}} \text{CH}_4 + \text{HCl}$
- **(b) Grignard's Reagent (RMgX)**:
  - Preparation: $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{Br} + \text{Mg} \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} \text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{MgBr}$
  - Reaction with Active Hydrogen:
    - $\text{CH}_3\text{MgBr} + \text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH} \to \text{CH}_4 + \text{Mg(OC}_2\text{H}_5\text{)Br}$
    - $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{MgBr} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \to \text{C}_2\text{H}_6 + \text{Mg(OH)Br}$
- **(c) Wurtz Reaction**:
  - $2\text{ R-X} + 2\text{Na} \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} \text{R-R} + 2\text{NaX}$ (Ethane and higher symmetrical alkanes).

### 2. From Unsaturated Hydrocarbons (Hydrogenation)
- $\text{CH}_2=\text{CH}_2 + \text{H}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{Ni or Pt / 573 K}} \text{CH}_3-\text{CH}_3$
- $\text{CH}\equiv\text{CH} + 2\text{H}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{Ni or Pt / 573 K}} \text{CH}_3-\text{CH}_3$
- Commercial application: Conversion of liquid vegetable oil to solid Vanaspati Ghee.

### 3. From Alcohols, Aldehydes and Ketones (Red P + HI at 423 K)
- Alcohol: $\text{ROH} + 2\text{HI} \xrightarrow{\text{red P / 423 K}} \text{RH} + \text{I}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$
- Aldehyde: $\text{RCHO} + 4\text{HI} \xrightarrow{\text{red P / 423 K}} \text{RCH}_3 + 2\text{I}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$
- Ketone: $\text{RCOR}' + 4\text{HI} \xrightarrow{\text{red P / 423 K}} \text{RCH}_2\text{R}' + 2\text{I}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$

### 4. From Carboxylic Acids (3 Routes)
- **(i) Soda Lime Decarboxylation**: $\text{RCOONa} + \text{NaOH} \xrightarrow[\Delta]{\text{CaO}} \text{RH} + \text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$ (1 carbon less).
- **(ii) Direct Reduction (Red P + 6 HI)**: $\text{RCOOH} + 6\text{HI} \xrightarrow{\text{red P / 423 K}} \text{RCH}_3 + 3\text{I}_2 + 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$ (Same carbon count).
- **(iii) Kolbe's Electrolysis**: Aqueous sodium salt on electrolysis yields higher even-carbon alkanes at the anode via free radical mechanism.
