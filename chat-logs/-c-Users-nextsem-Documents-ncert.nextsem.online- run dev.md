### **Q.14 (Groundwater & Infiltration Case Study)** [4 Marks]
*A remote village has a deep community well. Even during a minor drought when local rivers dry up, the well continues providing clear, cold water drawn from underground reservoirs stored in permeable rock layers.*  
**(a)** What is the process of surface water seeping through soil and rocks known as? (1 Mark)  
**(b)** Name the freshwater found trapped beneath the earth's surface in the spaces of soil and rock. (1 Mark)  
*(Attempt either c or d):*  
**(c)** Why does water drawn from very deep aquifers usually look clean and clear compared to muddy surface puddles? (2 Marks)  
*— OR —*  
**(d)** Describe any two ways that can be used to replenish depleted groundwater especially in urban areas. (2 Marks)  

<details>
<summary>👁️ Click to Reveal Answer / उत्तर देखें</summary>

> - **(a) Seepage Process (1 Mark):** **Infiltration (अंतःस्यंदन)**.
> - **(b) Name of Trapped Water (1 Mark):** **Groundwater (भौमजल)**; the water-bearing geological stratum is called an **Aquifer (जलभृत)**.

>   As surface water slowly percolates downwards through thick layers of soil, sand, gravel, and porous rock, these layers act as **natural physical filters**. ### **Q.15 (Speedometer & Motion Case Study)** [4 Marks]
*Ajay is sitting in the front seat of his father’s car during a highway trip. He watches a needle on the instrument panel fluctuating around 60. Right below it, a digital counter displays the total distance the car has travelled.*  
**(a)** Which instrument measures and displays the speed of a vehicle in km/hr? (1 Mark)  
**(b)** If the vehicle's odometer reading is 3850 km at the beginning of a journey and 4120 km at the end, calculate the total distance travelled during the trip. (1 Mark)  
*(Attempt either c or d):*  
**(c)** Write any one example each of uniform and non-uniform motion from day-to-day life. (2 Marks)  
*— OR —*  
**(d)** Distinguish between uniform and non-uniform motion using the example of Ajay’s car moving on a straight highway with no traffic and when his car is moving in city traffic. (2 Marks)  

<details>
<summary>👁️ Click to Reveal Answer / उत्तर देखें</summary>

> - **(a) Speed Measuring Instrument (1 Mark):** **Speedometer (चालमापी)**.
> - **(b) Distance Calculation (1 Mark):**  
>   $$\text{Distance} = \text{Final Reading} - \text{Initial Reading} = 4120\text{ km} - 3850\text{ km} = \mathbf{270\text{ km}}$$
> - **(c) Real-Life Examples (2 Marks):**  
>   - **Uniform Motion:** The movement of the tip of the second hand of a watch (constant angular speed covering equal distances in equal time).  
>   - **Non-Uniform Motion:** A bus travelling in congested traffic with frequent stops, acceleration, and deceleration.
> - **OR (d) Highway vs. City Traffic Distinction (2 Marks):**  
>   | Driving Scenario | Type of Motion | Scientific Explanation |
>   |---|---|---|
>   | **Straight highway with no traffic** | **Uniform Motion (एकसमान गति)** | The car maintains a constant speed (60 km/h) in a straight line, covering equal distances in equal intervals of time with zero acceleration. |
>   | **In city traffic** | **Non-Uniform Motion (असमान गति)** | The car repeatedly changes speed—braking, idling at traffic signals, accelerating—covering unequal distances in equal intervals of time. |
</details>
