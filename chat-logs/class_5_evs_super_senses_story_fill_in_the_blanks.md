# 🌿 Class 5 EVS Chapter 1 (Super Senses) Whole Story Fill-in-the-Blanks with Hints

## 📋 Session Overview
In this session, we built a comprehensive, interactive, line-by-line **Fill in the Blanks** whole story module in **English** based on **NCERT Class 5 Environmental Studies (EVS Looking Around Chapter 1: Super Senses / ehap101.pdf)** from the provided Google Drive source folder.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Implementation Details

### 1. Interactive Fill-in-the-Blanks Story Module ([`evs-ch1-super-senses-fill-in-the-blanks.html`](file:///c:/Users/manojkmr2403/Documents/ncert.nextsem.online/evs-ch1-super-senses-fill-in-the-blanks.html))
- **Coverage**: 100% textbook story coverage across **11 Detailed Sections** and **60 Numbered Blanks**.
- **Per-Blank Clues**:
  - `💡 Hint`: Dedicated contextual hint pill explaining the concept or clue.
  - `👁️ Ans`: Instant answer reveal pill with high-contrast amber styling.
- **Bulk Action Controls**:
  - `💡 Show All Hints`: Expands hints across all 11 sections.
  - `👁️ Show All Answers`: Expands answers across all 60 blanks for self-checking.
  - `🔒 Hide All`: Closes all dropdown pills back to test mode.
- **Story Sections Included**:
  1. *How Did the Ant Recognise a Friend? (Scent trails, guard ants, pheromones)*
  2. *The Scent of Dogs, Mosquitoes & Silkworms (Body heat, sole odor, territory marking)*
  3. *Good Smells, Bad Smells & Sushila’s Story (Sushila, baby Deepak, psychological perception)*
  4. *Eyes on the Sides vs Eyes in the Front (Bird eye sockets, neck movements, owls)*
  5. *Birds of Prey: Eagles, Kites & Vultures (4x vision magnification, spotting food from 2 km)*
  6. *Do Animals See Colours? (Daytime vision vs nocturnal black-and-white perception)*
  7. *Sharp Ears & Sound Vibrations (Snakes feeling ground tremors through skin)*
  8. *Warning Calls & Wildlife Danger Alarms (Langurs, bird alarms, 2004 Andaman tsunami, dolphin clicks)*
  9. *Slumber Secrets: The Sloth & Sleep Clocks (17 hours upside down, 40-year lifespan on 8 trees)*
  10. *The Tiger: The Ultimate Super Hunter (6x night vision, whiskers, rotatable ears, 3 km roar)*
  11. *Animals in Danger: Poaching & National Parks (Tusks, horns, skins, Jim Corbett & Ghana)*
